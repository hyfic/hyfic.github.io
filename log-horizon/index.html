What is Log Horizon?

A local desktop diary with two ways to write. Freewrite gives you a continuous page, while Pile collects individual thoughts into a chronological stream. Titles are optional.

Does it need an account or internet?

No. Your diary lives on your computer. There are no accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or AI services.

Can I keep an entry private?

Yes. Selected entries and entire piles can be encrypted with one diary password. Ordinary entries are not encrypted. Dates and the existence of private entries remain visible. There is no password reset, so keep your password safe.

Can I take my writing with me?

Export entries as Markdown, or save a full diary backup and restore it into an empty diary. Keep backups somewhere safe; there is no automatic cloud backup.
