Teal/green is the single interface accent, with neutral surfaces; this supersedes the earlier blue direction. Group the header links in a shared translucent bar. Use an airy Cofounder-inspired footer with a scenic postcard and irregular teal pixel ground. Keep the hero bright without a broad dark overlay. No decorative arrows. The original coastal artwork and an actual looping Snorlax video are central. Controls need consistent geometry and tactile micro-interactions, without title underline effects. All three app detail designs are approved and must remain unchanged.
