Frequently asked
questions

What is Manvalan Finance?

It’s a personal finance tracker for your everyday money. Record income and expenses, manage separate wallets, organize group ledgers, and keep track of money you lend or borrow.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account needed. Your records are stored locally, and everyday tracking works offline. There’s no backend, telemetry, or automatic cloud sync.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add transactions yourself, so you stay in control of what you record. Bank imports and automatic bank connections aren’t part of the app.

Can I track money I lend or borrow?

Yes. Record lent or borrowed amounts, add due dates, and track partial repayments or additional advances. Settled loans stay organized separately.

What happens if I change phones?

Export a full JSON backup in Settings, keep it somewhere safe, and restore it on your new device. Back up before uninstalling: records don’t sync automatically. CSV exports are for reading, not restoring.

Which devices can I use it on?

Manavalan Finance is built for Android. Download the Android APK from the v2.0.0 release on GitHub. There isn’t a public iOS download here yet.
